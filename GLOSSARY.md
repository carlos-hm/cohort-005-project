# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Support Staff**:
Cadence's own people who decide Refund Requests. Not to be confused with a Team, which is a customer that buys seats.
_Avoid_: Support team, the team, Team admin

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. Support Staff approve or decline it, and that decision is final. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Support Staff haven't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Support Staff have accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and the learner loses access to the course.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Support Staff have turned down, with a Decision Note. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Decision Note**:
Support Staff's message to the learner about how their Refund Request was decided. Required when declining, optional when approving.
_Avoid_: Decline reason, Resolution note

**Team Licence**:
A purchase of several seats in a course, made on behalf of a Team. It can't be refunded through a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund Support Staff have approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
